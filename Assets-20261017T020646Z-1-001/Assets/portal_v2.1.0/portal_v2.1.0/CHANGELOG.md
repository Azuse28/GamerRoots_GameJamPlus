# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-10

- Initial release - the rift portal: a torn hole with a spiral vortex turning inside it, a dark void at the far end of the tunnel and rays of energy licking out past a crackling rim
- 4 colours (normal, red, blue, green), 3 sizes, 16-frame loop that closes with zero differing pixels

## 2.0.0 - 2026-09-17

- New portal type - gate: a built gateway, three concentric rings turning at different speeds around a bright pulsing core
- The gate ships in two variants: plain, and _nofloor with no glow thrown on the ground beneath it
- Clip ids now name the portal type, so rift_blue_32 replaces portal_blue_32
- 24 clips: 2 types x 4 colours x 3 sizes, plus the gate's 12 nofloor variants

## 2.1.0 - 2026-09-17

- New portal type - weave: three rings of braided energy, two counter-rotating helices each, crossing at bright nodes
- The weave wears the same mouth, rim and rays as the rift, so the two swap without re-timing anything
- 36 clips: 3 types x 4 colours x 3 sizes, plus the gate's 12 nofloor variants
